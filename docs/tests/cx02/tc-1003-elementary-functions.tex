\section*{TC-1003 Elementary complex functions}
\textbf{Requirements:} TCC-CX-REQ-1003.

Evaluate every exponential, logarithmic, power, square-root, trigonometric,
inverse-trigonometric, hyperbolic, and inverse-hyperbolic family at controlled
finite points. Compare selected known values and inverse/identity relations
with precision-specific tolerances. Pass requires every family to link and
the numerical corpus to remain within tolerance.
